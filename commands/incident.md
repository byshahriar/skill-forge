---

Invoke the incident-response skill.

Iron law: mitigate first, diagnose second. Triage (is it real, who is affected, what changed), assign severity explicitly, restore service by the fastest safe route while preserving evidence, and communicate on a fixed interval in user terms. After resolution, verify recovery against the original symptom and write the blameless postmortem with owned, dated actions.
